i. Summary

MD candidate at the University of Münster, working at the intersection of cancer biology, molecular medicine, and clinical care.

Pursuing additional research training in Experimental Medicine alongside the medical curriculum. Previously a Visiting Student Researcher in Hani Goodarzi's lab at UCSF (Boehringer Ingelheim Fonds MD fellowship), studying RNA-binding proteins in breast cancer and designing and executing CRISPR-based functional screens related to metastasis biology.

Wet-lab methods include cell culture, molecular cloning, PCR, single-cell RNA-seq, and RNA-seq library preparation.
